Pop-ups

When a site wants to open a pop-up, you're asked, unless you've said for that site. Allowed pop-ups open in a tab of their own.

    No site has a setting yet: each asks.

    Videos

    A site's videos and sounds can be kept from playing: none of its pages' play, here or in the player. (In the ☰ menu, hold a video's Skip to block its site's.)

      Every site's videos play.

      Blocked sites

      Nothing is loaded from a blocked site or its subdomains: not its pages, nor its pictures, scripts or videos in other sites' pages.

        No site is blocked.

        What pages say

        As browsers do: the page's address to its own site, just the site to others, nothing from a secure page to an insecure one. Some sites refuse their pictures and videos without it.
